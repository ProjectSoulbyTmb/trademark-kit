# Mark Variants — Clearance & Decision (VOLTAGE THOTH)

We evaluated distinct variants of the "THOTH" name to dodge the crowded "THOTH"
field before any federal filing. See `CLEARANCE_SEARCH_FINDINGS.md` for the full
plain-"THOTH" conflict list (registered THOTH TECH LLC Serial 85298472;
siddsachar/Thoth local-first AI assistant; thoth-station; many others).

## Variants considered
| Variant          | Direct hit? | Risk        | Notes |
|------------------|-------------|-------------|-------|
| THOTH            | Yes (registered + many open-source) | HIGH | Senior registered THOTH TECH LLC (Class 9). §2(d) refusal likely. |
| THOTHMIND        | TAKEN       | N/A         | thothmind.app, thothmind.ai, thothmind.net, F6S listing. Do not use. |
| THOTHCORE        | No direct hit | Moderate  | Still THOTH-rooted; partial overlap with "core" kernel projects. |
| THOTH PRIME      | No direct hit | Moderate  | "Prime" is common in software; still THOTH-rooted. |
| **VOLTAGE THOTH**| No direct hit | LOW (er)   | Composite judged as a whole; "VOLTAGE" is our own OS brand. Distinct from all THOTH references found. |

## Decision
**Adopt VOLTAGE THOTH as the trademark.**
- The trademark is the composite "VOLTAGE THOTH", evaluated for confusion as a whole
  — not the substring "THOTH" alone. Adding VOLTAGE (our own local-first OS name)
  materially reduces likelihood-of-confusion risk versus plain "THOTH".
- "THOTH" remains the everyday name of the kernel; **VOLTAGE THOTH** is the mark we
  assert and would register. Use **VOLTAGE THOTH™** in public-facing materials.
- This is not a guarantee. A full knock-out search / attorney review is still
  recommended before paying USPTO fees, but the composite is a sound, lower-risk choice.
