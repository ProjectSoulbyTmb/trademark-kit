## Assessment
- **Mark strength:** "THOTH" (ancient-deity name) is *arbitrary/fanciful* for software
  → inherently strong. BUT because it's evocative, **many parties use "Thoth" for
  software**, so the field is crowded.
- **Closest live conflict:** **siddsachar/Thoth** ("Personal AI Sovereignty", local-first
  AI assistant) overlaps our THOTH (local-first autonomous kernel, AI-adjacent). Same
  mark + same broad software/AI category = elevated likelihood-of-confusion risk.
- **Registered senior mark:** **THOTH TECH LLC** (Class 9 educational software). A
  federal examiner can cite it under §2(d) against a plain "THOTH" application.
- **Consequence for plain THOTH:** a §1(a) application in Class 9/42 risks an office
  action for likelihood of confusion. → This is why we file as **VOLTAGE THOTH**.
