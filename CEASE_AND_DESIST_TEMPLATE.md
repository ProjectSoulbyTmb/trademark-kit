# Cease & Desist Letter Template (Trademark, self-written, $0)

Copy this into an email or letter. Fill the [bracketed] fields. No attorney
required for a common-law ™ claim — but keep a copy and proof of sending.

---

**Subject:** Trademark infringement — [YourSoftwareName]™

Dear [Infringer Name / Company],

We are the owner of common-law trademark rights in the name
"[YourSoftwareName]"™, which we have used in commerce in connection with
[software / SaaS / app] since [First Use Date].

Your use of a confusingly similar name "[TheirName]" in the same market is
likely to cause consumer confusion and infringes our rights.

We request that you, within **30 days** of this letter:
  (a) cease all use of the infringing name, and
  (b) confirm in writing that you will do so.

We hope to resolve this amicably and without litigation.

Sincerely,
[Your Name / Company]
[Email / Address]
[Date]

---

## Sending notes
- Send from an email/account that timestamps the message (proof of delivery).
- Keep the sent copy + any reply in your trademark folder.
- If they ignore or push back, consider a short attorney consult before escalating.
